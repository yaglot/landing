About Yaglot

What does Yaglot mean?

Yaglot brings together ya for you and glot for language. Put together, it's about you speaking many languages.

What is Yaglot?

Yaglot is a new way to learn languages. Our lessons focus on the language you actually need in daily life, explain why the language works the way it does, and adapt as you learn so you can focus on what matters most.

Which languages can I learn?

We're starting with Mandarin for English speakers, in both Traditional and Simplified Chinese. Next, we'll help Mandarin speakers learn English, with more languages on the way.

Do I need any experience?

No. Yaglot is designed to be open to everyone. Complex ideas are broken down into simple, clear explanations, so you can start with confidence.

Who is behind Yaglot?

Yaglot is made by Yaglot Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. Our team has PhDs in Cognitive Science and Language Science, and their research on how we learn and use language shapes every unit.
