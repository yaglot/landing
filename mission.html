Our mission

We're transforming the way people learn languages, so that learning makes sense, fits real life and feels natural.

Why we're here

Learning a language is more than memorising words and rules. It's about unlocking a new way to think and express yourself, and connecting with the people and culture behind the language.

Starting with Mandarin

Our first product helps English speakers learn Mandarin, in both Traditional and Simplified Chinese. Next, we'll help Mandarin speakers learn English, with more languages on the way.

What we believe

Learning should adapt to you

We adapt as you learn, so every step focuses on what matters most for you.

Understanding beats memorising

When you know why the language works the way it does, the patterns make sense and stay with you.

Real life comes first

We teach the language you actually need, so you can use it with people straight away.

Encouragement keeps you going

A supportive, step-by-step approach keeps learning motivating, scientific and fun.

Everyone should be able to learn

Complex ideas are broken down into simple, clear explanations that anyone can follow.

Research leads the way

Our work is grounded in Cognitive Science and Language Science, the study of how we learn and use language.
