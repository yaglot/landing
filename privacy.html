Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

Yaglot Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, runs yaglot.com. We decide how the information described here is used, which makes us its 'data controller'. If you have any questions, email us at hello@yaglot.com.

What we collect

When you join our waitlist:

  • your email address
  • the type of device you used: computer, tablet or phone
  • where you came from, for example LinkedIn, Google or 'direct'
  • your browser language, for example en-GB
  • your time zone, for example Europe/London
  • the date and time you joined, and which version of this notice you saw

When you contact us using our form:

  • your name and email address
  • the topic you choose and your message
  • the date and time you sent it, and which version of this notice you saw

We only ask for your name when you contact us, and we don't record your exact location.

Why we use it

  • to email you about Yaglot's launch and early access
  • to reply to your message
  • to understand, in general terms, where our visitors come from and which devices and languages they use, so we can improve our website and plan our courses

For the waitlist, we rely on your consent, which you give when you join. You can withdraw it at any time. When you contact us, we use your details to reply to you, because you've asked us to.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We keep the waitlist and your messages in Google Sheets, a service run by Google, which stores them for us. Google may store them on servers outside the UK.

Our website is hosted by GitHub and uses Google Fonts, which receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

Cookies

We don't use cookies, and we don't store anything on your device.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them. We keep messages for as long as we need to answer them and deal with any follow-up, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop emailing you

Just email hello@yaglot.com. Every email we send you will include a way to unsubscribe.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top.
