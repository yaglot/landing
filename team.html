Our team

Yaglot was founded by Dr Chiawei Wang, an applied language scientist with a PhD in Linguistics from the University of Edinburgh, where he trained to find out how language works in our brains and in the world around us. Now he's putting that science to work, with a team of experts in learning science, design and technology.

How science shapes Yaglot

Science in, fun out. Our research shapes every step of the way, so you learn naturally from day one.

Where we are

We're a remote team, working from wherever we do our best thinking. You'll meet more of us here soon.
