Starting with Mandarin

Our first product is designed to help English speakers learn Mandarin. Both Traditional and Simplified Chinese versions are set to launch soon. Next, we'll expand our mission to help Mandarin speakers learn English, with support for more languages on the way.

What makes us different

Tailored guidance

We adapt as you learn, and that is what sets us apart. Our feedback keeps you focused on what matters most, helping you strengthen your skills and build on your progress. Focus on the areas that need extra attention, so every step of your learning journey truly counts.

Fascinating fun facts

Mandarin is full of little surprises, and each one has a reason behind it. Discover why the language works the way it does, from quirky grammar to the stories hidden inside characters, so the patterns finally make sense and stay with you.

Real-world skills

The focus is entirely on the language you actually need for daily life. You will learn how to navigate social situations and connect with locals confidently. Every lesson provides meaningful skills you can put into practice straight away.

Encouraging approach

Learning a language is more than just memorising words and rules. It is about unlocking a new way to think and express yourself. This supportive method guides you step by step, keeping you motivated to explore the language and culture in a scientific yet fun way.

Open to everyone

Language learning should be accessible to everyone. The entire programme is designed from the ground up to be inclusive, welcoming and straightforward. Complex ideas are broken down into simple, clear explanations that are easy to follow, so you feel confident.

Built by experts

Behind Yaglot is a team with PhDs in Cognitive Science and Language Science, who study how the brain picks up language and how we use it in everyday life. Their research shapes every unit, from your very first words to real conversations, so you can learn naturally, just as you did as a child.
